01 / About Me

About
Me

I’m Millie Jones, a graphic designer based in Hampshire. My main focus is creating and applying brand identities, making sure they work well across different types of design.

Other work includes digital content such as social media posts and billboards, along with print designs like posters and leaflets. My preferred style is bold, flat graphics with bright colours, using simple but eye-catching visuals.

I enjoy experimenting with creative ideas and making designs that stand out. When I encounter design-based problems, I think creatively to find a solution.
